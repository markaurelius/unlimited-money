# The Pitch

> Write a rough description of your idea here — one paragraph to a few sentences is enough.
> Don't polish it. Stream of consciousness is fine. Then run `make interview`.

I want this agent, with all of its startup expertise, and awareness of the world (do research as necessary), to determine what the lowest-touch approach would be to building a project that could generate actual revenue. Look for gaps in markets, arbitrage opportunities, areas where accessible data is under-utilized, places where margins were previously not good enough, but where significantly reduced software costs actually makes those margins favorable for a single person entrepreneur.
